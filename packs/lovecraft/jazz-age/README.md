# Lovecraftian investigations in the Jazz Age (OCF pack)

The character rules of an any-time Lovecraftian investigation game, as its OGL System Reference
Document localizes them for the Jazz Age (the world between the World Wars). The document's name
is its publisher's Product Identity: this pack uses a generic name (`pack.yaml` and `LICENSE` hold
the license, its section 15 and the Product Identity statement).

- six statistics (3 to 18, tested at ×5), an upbringing's harshness (STR and CON bonuses, lower
  starting SAN, adversity skills); Hit Points, Willpower Points, Sanity, Breaking Point, Maximum
  and Recovery SAN, the damage bonus;
- 44 skills with their base ratings, every one on the sheet; typed skills (Science, Foreign
  Language…) with a specialty, another specialty a copy more;
- 11 Jazz Age archetypes (their skills, those to choose, Resources and Bonds);
- Resources (at hand, stowed, in storage, check boxes) and Bonds (individual and community);
- tests on 1D100 (critical on 01 or matching digits, fumble on 100 or matching digits), stat,
  Luck and Resources tests; Willpower breakdowns;
- combat: attacks with the weapon's skill, damage and the damage bonus, Lethality, armor and armor
  piercing; the example weapons and armor; healing;
- Sanity tests and losses, the Breaking Point and disorders;
- creation, guided and random (`create-protagonist`).

Not yet modelled: opposed tests and pursuits, the effects of disorders, adapting and projecting onto
Bonds, community bond trade-ins, downtime, rituals and supernatural effects, creatures.
