# SagaS Core (OCF pack)

From the _Rules of SagaS 0.995 LLL (OGL)_:

- characteristics, secondary characteristics, characteristic bonus (Universal Table), shock
  thresholds, physical and psychical hit points, injuries and stun, impact bonus, Reflex Dodge;
- 119 skills (section 17): the 108 listed and an "Other" skill per characteristic, whose initial
  level (-15, -10, -5 or 0) is chosen when learned; generic skills are taken once per
  specialty; 62 weapons (14.1, 14.2) and 14 protections (15);
- creation points (150/250/350, costs per band, x3 for secondary characteristics, skills);
- actions: `characteristic-check`, `skill-check`, `opposed-check` (2d6* roll under, degree, natural 2 / 12,
  automatic failure, difficulty and situation degree modifiers), `damage` (dice capped at the weapon's
  DMAX), `take-damage` (protection less armor piercing, stun, shock),
  `rest-short`;
- creation starts (`create-character`), guided and random: power level, social class and the
  universe's technological level, then main and secondary characteristics bought with the
  points, skills learned and raised one step at a time, weapons and armor. Generic and "Other"
  skills (a specialty or an initial level to give) are left to the sheet. While the character is
  being created, the points spent may not exceed those available.
